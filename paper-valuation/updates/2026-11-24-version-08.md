## 3. What we got wrong

1. **The forecast rose 464% in seven days, after falling 95% in six.** Version 7 published base $0.00070 on 17 Nov; the same method today gives $0.0039. Version 7 said the refill counted twice; the bounce counts twice too: the reference alone moves the base +238%, the share +69%. Had a version run on each day from 17 to 23 Nov, the method would have published a base anywhere from $0.00052 to $0.015, 29× apart; over the last 13 closing days the three-day reference ranged from $0.043M to $0.78M, 18× (our arithmetic). Over the same week the longer windows barely moved: both inputs over seven days give $0.0074 (Version 7's shadow: $0.0085, −13%), over fourteen $0.0079 (was $0.0090, −12%). The method stands until Insaaf decides the window on 30 Nov (BOARD 18).
2. **Version 7's refill estimate was six times too slow.** It said the pool needed about $1.0M of net trader losses and, at the 14–17 Nov pace, about 12 days. It took two: $4.87M on the 18 Nov row and the bar on 19 Nov; share-80-v8 hit on 19 Nov at 95%. Losses come in lumps, not at an average pace.
3. **Two of its published readings were overwritten.** Version 7 printed house money $7.22M and open bets $31.7M long, $11.3M short for 17 Nov (the desk's 07:04 UTC read). The job's file now holds a 15:55 UTC read of that day ($7.56M; $24.6M and $7.1M), because a later run replaces the day's row, and the charts draw the later one. The same happened to 21 Nov, whose morning read had the bets leaning short (−0.94×) and the pool at the bar. Keeping the first read is Insaaf's call (BOARD 23).
4. **"House money" is still undefined.** It is the pool plus PaperDash's "reserve", which moved $3.19M (17 Nov) → $4.16M (21 Nov) → $3.40M today with no documented source; PaperDash's `summary.balances.reserve` reads 0 while `summary.paper.reserve` reads $3.40M (sweep). 5. **A data promise kept late, one not kept.** The daily timelock read, promised with Versions 5, 6 and 7, was built with this version (live from the 25 Nov job). The direct-chain fallback for when PaperDash fails (BOARD 31) is not built. ## The throttle, five weeks on

Insaaf's thesis (17 Oct 2026): traders must be able to get in and out at size; fees are a negligible part of a trader's decision; a platform should put trading and the house before LP rewards; "if the house and trading experience are in order, rewards will follow". No new thoughts from him reached this version.

- **Agrees:** rewards followed the house. Once the pool was whole again on 19 Nov, stakers got 95%, 98% and 93% of $2.35M in three days; the call made on 17 Nov hit; the queue stayed $0. The lean stayed under 15× on every read since 22 Oct (at most 3.9× since set 3; 2.21× today).
- **Disagrees:** the refill is the default rule at a $5M bar, not a choice, and the bar has not moved in 38 days; the cycle repeated within two days (back to the fee split on 22–23 Nov). Trading has not grown into the space: open bets $26.2M against $71.5M on 11 Nov, open positions 650 against 1,011, money on platform $20.7M. The team's own backstop shrank from $3.65M to about $0.9M.
- **What it means for the number:** the forecast swings with the bar cycle, not with the thesis: the three-day method gave a base between $0.00052 and $0.015 on the seven days 17–23 Nov, while the seven- and fourteen-day windows moved about 12%. The window is Insaaf's call on 30 Nov.
