**Version 3 · 20 Oct 2026 · Tracking closest to: bull on revenue, by a closing margin, too early to count · Calls so far: 4 hit, 2 missed, 2 on track, 3 off track · Next version: 27 Oct**

What we got wrong: the reference caught the peak, the 17 Oct tell was a dip between sweeps, and two hits rest on a rule that measures the fee split. Next: Version 4 on 27 October, weekly through November, and a version within a week of the upgrade going live or the bar moving.

## 3. What we got wrong

1. **The reference caught the peak.** Version 2's three-day reference ($6.29M, 14–16 Oct) was the three best days after launch day; three days later the same method reads $1.72M. The method re-bases on the newest three days, so the forecast swung +40% and then −70%. Consequence in numbers: base $0.11 → $0.034. A seven-day reference (13–19 Oct, $4.08M) would give bull $0.47, base $0.074, bear $0.0086 (section 6 of the arithmetic: a shadow run, not the forecast). Whether the reference should widen is a question of logic for Insaaf; the method stands until he decides. The desk's pick: keep the three-day reference while the rush fades (changing it now, right when it hurts, would look like patching), and decide at the 30 Nov version with six weeks of data.
2. **The 17 Oct tell was a dip between sweeps, not a pause.** The addendum said stakers "have had only the 1% win fee since about midnight UTC". The pool reads under the bar whenever traders are ahead on open bets (trackedLp = funds + open house PnL); it crossed back over the bar in three hours that day (sweeps of $22K, $324K and $251K) and stakers received $783,566, 87% of the day's revenue. Consequence: the "first tell against the base case" was not one; the base case came down three days later for a different reason (revenue).
3. **Two hits rest on a rule that measures the wrong thing.** `restart-base` and `payout-restart` marked themselves hit on 18 Oct from holders ÷ revenue (90%). PaperDash's daily allocation to stakers equals DefiLlama's holders line to the dollar, so the dollars are real, but the ratio is the split of distributed revenue between stakers and the treasury; it read 85–95% on every day since launch, including 17 Oct. And the bar never doubled, so there was nothing to restart. The hits stand as the rule scored them: nothing on the page is hand-edited. Lined up for Insaaf: (a) re-score both as missed (the premise failed) or leave the hits with the note; (b) the replacement rule for the next bar raise: the pool reading at the new bar on the daily contract read (`lp` ≥ 98% of `cap`), which cannot be satisfied while a refill is running. Desk's pick: leave the hits as scored with the note, retire the ratio rule, and add the pool-at-the-bar rule when the bar moves.
4. **The test was written as if the doubling were certain.** The tracking card said "from 17 Oct the pool refills to $10M first", the key dates carried refill dates from 17 Oct, and the Version 2 moment on the record is titled "Payout bar doubles". It did not happen. The card and the key dates now follow the contract read; the moment stays as the record of what was expected that day, with the 18 Oct moment "Bar did not double" next to it.
5. **Two small errors in the record.** The call sheet's static fallback text said "+36%" where the computed figure was +40% (the page corrected itself on load; the fallback is now written from the arithmetic). A forwarded desktop note put 16 Oct revenue at $6.37M against the API's $5.45M (caught on 17 Oct). Neither reached the model.
6. **A doubt resolved.** The docs can be read this time: `tailProgressUsd` is "a high-water-mark that never decreases" of cumulative LP gain past the $2M threshold (docs.papertrade.xyz/how/mint-curve/), which confirms Version 2's inference that the curve runs on gross flow. The supply tuning reads 4.2 today (4.0 on 17 Oct); the ratio drifts up because the mark grows with gross flow while revenue is net.
7. **The sweep of 17 Oct missed a 16 Oct post** (the weekend-maintenance note, 19:56 UTC on 16 Oct). It is in the 20 Oct note's timeline and bears on the 17 Oct fall (leverage and open-interest caps are cut around Hyperliquid's maintenance window).

## 6. What would change the call

- **Toward bull:** revenue holding above $1.7M a day (today's reference) through November, so the weekly re-runs stop re-basing down, then above $0.58M a day for 30 days after the mint rate is under 10 per $1; money on platform back above $138M by 30 Nov; a dated transfer plan, listing or distribution deal.
- **Toward bear:** revenue below $0.63M a day (Version 2's base bar) for 30 days; the bets leaning more than 15× the house money on a closed day; an admin cut or pause of staker payouts; live supply above 10B before January 2027.
- **Triggers for a version, either way:** the payout bar raised to $10M or above (the refill pause and the restart test start from that day); the timelocked upgrade going live (the mint rule changes, so the supply curve in the model is re-read from the contract and the change is named in the log); any official date for transferability.
