## 3. What we got wrong

1. **The reference slid again.** Version 4 asked whether revenue would "hold near $0.7M a day". It did not: 7–9 Nov averaged $0.44M, 36% lower, so the base fell 35%. The reference alone moves it −33.7%; the higher share (+2.1%) and more supply (−3.5%, starting supply and mint basis together) roughly cancel. A seven-day reference (3–9 Nov, $0.4663M) gives $0.0100 against the method's $0.0094, so this week the window matters little.
2. **A run that did not happen shows how fragile the reference is.** Had the 3 Nov run started, it would have read 31 Oct–2 Nov ($19,262, $19,773, $74,658: $0.0379M a day, 50% to stakers) and published a base of **$0.0004** (bull $0.0030, bear $0.00005), a 21st of today's $0.0094, which the same method reached one week later (section 9 of the arithmetic, with Version 5's supply inputs). A year-out forecast that moves 20× on which three days a run lands is measuring the week. The method stands until Insaaf decides the window (BOARD 18); this is the strongest evidence yet for a longer one.
3. **We were a week late.** Version 5 was due on 3 Nov. That run, and the 3–5 Nov daily checks, stopped at once on the desk's weekly usage limit; the page showed "Version 5 · 3 Nov" as upcoming for a week after the date. The data job kept writing, so no reading was lost; the record now carries the miss on 3 Nov.
4. **"Tracking closest to: bull" still says bull by construction.** Each case's bar falls with the reference, so recent revenue keeps clearing the bull bar ($149K now). Against Version 1's fixed bars the last seven days ($0.47M a day) sit almost exactly on the base bar ($0.44M). The page keeps its rule until Insaaf decides (BOARD 18).
5. **Two data fixes promised with Version 5 are not done.** The job's `mint_rate` still prints the retired curve (9.80 against 10.22 by the live rule; the page computes the right figure), and there is no daily timelock read, so a change to the payout bar would reach us on the day it acts, not 24 hours ahead (BOARD 25, 26, re-dated).
6. **The live Version 4 page changed after publication.** The 30 Oct note was dated, labelled and moved no number, and the original was kept at /v4/; but the rule is that a published version is not edited and a correction is a later version. Both states are now kept, /v4/ and /v4/addendum/.
7. **Small errors.** The "What moved" table on the Version 4 page gave its market bet as $0.0030 per PAPER; $25M over 9.03B is $0.0028, as its sheet and its note said. "By Oct 2027" is loose: the page projects 361 days from each version's date (from 10 Nov 2026 to 6 Nov 2027); on a fixed 10 Oct 2027 the bull case is $0.056 instead of $0.055, base and bear unchanged. The page formula stays (a change would be a method change); BOARD 27 closes with this explanation. The research home page described Version 4 with the old-curve comparison figures ($0.015 / $0.095 / $0.0016) instead of the published $0.014 / $0.079 / $0.0015 from 27 Oct to 10 Nov; it now carries Version 5's figures.
8. **Our 27 Oct sweep missed a second upgrade.** The SessionKeyManager (session keys) was upgraded through the timelock (queued 22 Oct 22:09, executed 24 Oct 09:22 UTC), with no post and no line in the docs' change log. It does not touch the mint, the bar or the Exchange, so no number moves, but Version 4 said the 21 Oct upgrade was the only change. This sweep read every timelock event since 20 Oct (and decoded the two 20 Oct events Version 4 left open: the 24-hour delay being set, and a cancelled earlier operation).

## The throttle, three weeks on

Insaaf's thesis (17 Oct 2026): traders must be able to get in and out at size; fees are a negligible part of a trader's decision; a platform should put trading and the house before LP rewards; "if the house and trading experience are in order, rewards will follow". House first is bearish for PAPER for a few months and bullish for next year; the base case is the house-first world. No new thoughts from him reached this version (`bin/user-messages-since 2026-10-17T12:10:00Z` returned only a task notification; inbox/ empty apart from pdf-download/).

The desk's tempering, with three weeks of daily contract reads: the house held. The queue read $0 on every day, including 5 Nov, when house money fell to $5.91M while the bets leaned 6.6× it, and the lean has stayed under 15× on every read since 22 Oct. But the dial still reads rewards first: the bar is still $5M, 24 days after the raise to $10M was due, and the pool keeps sweeping above it while the reserve sits at $3.46M against $5.00M on 20 Oct. Trading has not grown into the space either: open bets $164M → $80.6M, open positions 1,549 → 1,076, money on platform $31.5M → $24.2M since Version 4. The lean is low because the bets shrank. Bearish now, as the thesis says; "rewards will follow" is still untested, because house first has not been tried.
