## 4. What we got wrong, and what changed under us

1. **The reference run-rate was built on figures the source later revised.** On 14 October DefiLlama showed 12 Oct at $4.65M and 13 Oct at $6.08M. It now shows $3.93M and $4.52M. The same three days give $3.69M a day, 17% below the frozen $4.45M. Same method on the revised figures: bull $0.41, base $0.069, bear $0.0091 (−15%). The call stands as made; the error is noted here and in the page's tracker, which already flags revisions.
2. **The supply tuning was too low.** The model set the curve's high-water mark at 3.0 × cumulative revenue. The live contract shows $222.25M against $55.18M of revenue, a ratio of about 4.0, and 8.45B PAPER against the model's 7.16B for today (−15%). Frozen inputs with the ratio at 4.0: bull $0.48, base $0.078, bear $0.0096 (−2% to −8%). Our best reading of why: the curve counts gross flow into the pool, not net losses. That is an inference from the contract's `tailProgress` field; the docs still cannot be read as text.
3. **The 17 October test was timed from each case's run-rate, not from the actual one.** At today's pace the pool refills in about a day, so the first reading will not separate bull from base from bear. The reading that counts is how long surplus payouts stay paused once the rush fades, from mid-November on. The test keeps its dates; its first reading is downgraded to a robustness check.
4. **Two doubts in the report are resolved, and neither was a model input.** The "$910B daily volume" on 13 Oct was cumulative; PaperDash's hourly data gives $73.7B for that day. The 30.8 PAPER per $1 reading that did not fit the published curve fits it once the curve runs on gross flow (point 2).
5. **"Market bet $0.016" divided $100M by 6.1B modelled supply.** On live supply the same strike is $0.012, and the new median near $200M is $0.024.
