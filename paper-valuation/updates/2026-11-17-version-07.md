## 3. What we got wrong

1. **The forecast fell 95% in six days.** Version 6 published base $0.015 on 11 Nov; the same method today gives $0.00070. The three-day window landed on a refill: after the 13 Nov payout the pool sat under the bar, so revenue fell to $59K a day and the stakers' share to 51%. Both inputs come from the same three days, so the refill counted twice: reference −91.3%, share −47.4%. With both inputs over seven days (10–16 Nov: $0.4181M, 93%) the base is $0.0085; over fourteen days (3–16 Nov: $0.4422M, 93%), $0.0090. Over the last 13 closing days the three-day reference ranged from $0.059M to $0.751M, 12.7×. The method stands until Insaaf decides (BOARD 18, 30 Nov). The desk's pick sharpens: whatever window he picks should cover at least one refill, so at least fourteen days, and should apply to the share as well as the reference.
2. **Version 6's question was answered no.** It asked whether revenue would hold near $0.6M a day by 17 Nov. The seven-day average is $0.42M, the three-day $0.059M.
3. **We misread "the pool above the bar", and "house money" contains a number we cannot define.** The 13 Nov daily check reported the pool at $5.55M, $0.55M above the bar (`excess` $550,457), and asked whether set 3 had changed the sweep. It was staker rewards booked in the early hours and not yet paid out (`pendingRewards` $560,958), not new house money. Separately, the house figure the page and the throttle tell use (pool + PaperDash's `reserve`) has included `reserve` since Version 3 without a definition. It fell $4.49M → $3.79M (13 → 14 Nov), about $0.70M of the reported "house −$2.49M", and is $3.19M today. The label stays this version so the series and the skew-15 call stay comparable; defining or dropping it goes on the board for Version 8.
4. **The backstop we described shrank.** Version 6 called the team's $5.36M in HyperCore lending a second hand on the dial (`donateToLp`). No donation came. On 15 Nov the multisig withdrew $1.71M: $1.67M to a signer and on to Arbitrum, $50K to its merchandise company's wallet; $3.65M remains. Whether that is fee income or reserves is not established.
5. **One data fix done, one still not.** BOARD 25 is done with this version: the job's `mint_rate` computes supply × `rebateC36` ÷ 1e36 from the 17 Nov row (10.36), earlier rows keep the retired curve's 9.80. Small: Version 6 put live supply at 10B around 18 Dec; at the last six days' pace (17.8M a day) it is about 20 Dec (our arithmetic).

## The throttle, a month on

Insaaf's thesis (17 Oct 2026): traders must be able to get in and out at size; fees are a negligible part of a trader's decision; a platform should put trading and the house before LP rewards; "if the house and trading experience are in order, rewards will follow". House first is bearish for PAPER for a few months and bullish for next year; the base case is the house-first world. No new thoughts from him reached this version.

The desk's tempering: the house paid the largest single win on file ($2,137,629 to one wallet on 13 Nov) on the spot, the queue stayed at $0, and the lean has stayed under 15× on every read since 22 Oct (at most 3.9× since set 3; 2.83× today). The week after was house first in action: for three days stakers' income went into rebuilding the pool. But that is the default rule at a $5M bar, not a choice; the bar has not moved, 31 days after the raise was due. Trading has not grown into the space: open bets $71.5M → $43.0M since 11 Nov, open positions 1,011 → 848, money on platform $24.0M → $21.3M. Set 3 adds an execution risk the thesis cares about: the platform's USDC now sits in HyperCore lending and is withdrawn when a winner is paid (same-second on 16 Nov; a squeeze in that market could delay a large win, our inference). The forecast took the thesis's short-term bearishness at full force: a refill week sent the three-day reference to $59K and the base to $0.00070. "Rewards will follow" gets its first test this week, in the new call.
